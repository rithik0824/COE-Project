PROJECT PRESENTATION · SEM 5 IE28

Live ETA Communication & Technician Assignment System

Solving inaccurate customer arrival estimates in field-service companies by implementing dynamic skill-based matching, honest uncertainty ranges, and a 5,000+ dataset benchmark.

Target Industry

Field-service operations (AC Repair, Electrical, Plumbing, Appliance Service).

Key Innovation

Dynamic ETA range model (e.g. 35–50 mins) replacing false single-number precision.

Scale Benchmark

5,006 jobs & 200 technicians simulated locally with SQLite audit traceability.

PROBLEM DEFINITION

The Field-Service ETA Failure Problem

Why traditional dispatching leads to poor customer experience and operational overhead:

1. False Precision

Traditional systems promise an exact arrival time (e.g., "Arriving at 10:15 AM"), ignoring traffic, dwell times, and prior job delays.

2. Static Non-Updating ETAs

When a technician gets delayed in rush-hour traffic or an appointment overruns, customer ETAs do not update in real time.

3. High Call Center Load

Frustrated customers make repeated "Where is my technician?" status-check enquiries, ballooning support costs.

4. Skill Mismatch Risk

Manual dispatching can send technicians lacking the specific required skill, causing wasted visits and unfulfilled jobs.

SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

Proposed Solution Architecture

1. Automated Assignment

Filters available technicians by required skill (AC, Electrical, Plumbing, etc.) and auto-selects the nearest technician using Haversine distance.

2. Dynamic Range Engine

Calculates travel time considering time-of-day traffic skew and previous job overruns to output an honest eta_low – eta_high uncertainty range.

3. Dispatcher Traceability

Provides full manual override controls, warning flags for skill mismatch, and persistent timestamped logs in eta_history & assignment_history.

TECH STACK: Python 3 + Flask Backend | SQLite Database (Zero-Config) | Vanilla JS & Bootstrap 5 UI | 100% Local & Offline
ENGINE MATHEMATICS

Simulated ETA Calculation Engine

Distance & Travel Time

Haversine Formula: Calculates great-circle distance between technician (lat1, lng1) and customer (lat2, lng2).
Base Time = (Distance / 24 km/h) * 60 mins

Traffic & Overrun Factors

Traffic Multipliers: Light (1.0x), Moderate (1.3x), Heavy (1.7x) based on appointment hour.
Job Overrun: Adds +12 to +28 mins if previous job delayed.

Honest Uncertainty Band Formula

Uncertainty Width = 10 + Traffic Extra (4–8m) + Overrun Extra (10m)
ETA Low = Max(5, Travel_Mins - Uncertainty/2)  |  ETA High = Travel_Mins + Uncertainty/2

DEMO VERIFICATION

4 Core Required Demo Edge Cases

Case 1: Heavy Traffic

Rush hour appointment automatically widens the ETA uncertainty range to inform customer upfront.

Case 2: Technician Unavailable

When all skilled technicians are on leave, job status switches to unassigned for dispatcher intervention.

Case 3: Service Job Overrun

Assigned technician is delayed at a prior address; system re-calculates ETA and logs slip reason in history timeline.

Case 4: Skill Mismatch Override

Dispatcher manually overrides with nearest general tech; system records assignment but displays prominent warning flag.

RISK MANAGEMENT

Failure Mode & Failure Analysis

Failure Mode: Double Booking

Mitigation: Availability status switches to busy immediately upon auto-assignment or manual override.

Failure Mode: Skill Gap Unavailability

Mitigation: System flags job as unassigned with human-readable reason rather than silent failure.

Failure Mode: Unexpected Traffic Spike

Mitigation: Dynamic resimulation route allows manual/triggered ETA recalibration and historical reason logging.

Failure Mode: Unauthorized Override

Mitigation: Every dispatcher action is immutably logged in assignment_history with dispatcher notes.
