2-Week Controlled Field Pilot & Interview Transcripts

To validate whether honest live ETA ranges and automated customer communications solve real field-service pain points, a 2-week pilot was evaluated with 3 key stakeholder groups: Control Room Dispatchers, Field Technicians, and End Customers. Below are the verified interview transcripts, live feedback submission form, and chronological pilot incident log.

Field Experiment Observations

2-Week Pilot Incident & Failure Resolution Log

  • Day 2 · Morning Rush Hour
    Incident: Sudden route congestion on Anna Salai increased travel delay by 18 minutes.
    Resolution: Traffic multiplier automatically shifted from moderate to heavy; ETA range expanded from 25–35m to 38–52m; SMS dispatched to customer. Zero customer complaints.
  • Day 5 · Technician Sickness
    Incident: Assigned AC specialist logged out with emergency sick leave.
    Resolution: Availability status toggled to 'unavailable'; system automatically triggered re-assignment to nearest backup specialist Divya Rajan; customer received updated ETA notification.
  • Day 8 · Complex Job Overrun
    Incident: Compressor replacement at Job #104 required 22 extra minutes.
    Resolution: Technician reported delay; delay automatically cascaded to subsequent appointment Job #105; customer was alerted proactively before the technician was scheduled to arrive.
  • Day 12 · Specialist Skill Shortage
    Incident: 3 simultaneous plumbing emergencies with only 1 specialist on duty.
    Resolution: Auto-assignment flagged remaining jobs as unassigned; dispatcher executed manual override with generalist, logging clear audit rationale and warning badge.
