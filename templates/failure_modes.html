Failure Mode 1 Route Bottlenecks

Peak-Hour Traffic Surge & Gridlock

Context: Arterial corridors (e.g. Mount Road / Anna Salai) experience sudden rush-hour congestion that doubles travel time.
Baseline Behavior: Naive static 35-min estimate remains frozen. Customer expects arrival at 09:35 AM; tech arrives at 09:58 AM.
Proposed Architecture: Incorporates time-of-day multipliers (1.7x). Uncertainty band widens to [30–45m] or [38–54m]. Proactive SMS alert sent automatically.
Why Appropriate: Replaces false certainty with an honest range that accommodates urban traffic variance without breaching expectations.

Failure Mode 2 Resource Outage

Technician Unavailability & Breakdown

Context: Field technician experiences vehicle breakdown or illness while assigned to active jobs.
Baseline Behavior: System enters undefined state; job remains assigned to unavailable technician.
Proposed Architecture: Event handler intercepts status shift. Automatically migrates active jobs to nearest free specialist. If no specialist is free, cleanly marks job as 'unassigned' with clear reason logging.
Why Appropriate: Graceful degradation guarantees zero zombie assignments and gives dispatchers immediate escalation visibility.

Failure Mode 3 Cascading Overrun

Dwell Time & Service Overruns

Context: Complicated AC motor replacement at Job A takes 75 minutes instead of 40 minutes.
Baseline Behavior: Job B customer receives no alert; waits at home wondering why technician is 35 minutes late.
Proposed Architecture: Cascading delay listener adds +15 to +25 min delay buffer and expands uncertainty band by +10 min. Sends automated delay notification citing prior job overrun.
Why Appropriate: Field routing is fundamentally sequential; modeling dependencies eliminates the silent delay trap.

Failure Mode 4 Emergency Governance

Skill Mismatch & Dispatcher Manual Override

Context: Emergency plumbing leak occurs, but all certified plumbers are booked. A general technician is 200m away.
Baseline Behavior: Rigid rejection or silent assignment with zero legal/audit accountability.
Proposed Architecture: Dispatcher override route displays warning banner, requires mandatory reason input, and immutably logs action in assignment_history.
Why Appropriate: Delivers human-in-the-loop operational agility while preserving 100% auditability for regulatory compliance.
