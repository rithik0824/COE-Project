1 Executive Summary & Core Value Proposition

Conventional field service platforms commit to a single point-estimate (e.g. "Arriving at 10:15 AM"). Because urban traffic, unpredictable prior repair complexities (dwell times), and technician availability shocks are inherently stochastic, point-estimates frequently fail. This triggers high customer friction and floods support desks with "Where Is My Operator?" (WISMO) calls.

This project delivers an autonomous Live ETA Communication and Technician Assignment System communicating an honest dynamic arrival uncertainty interval (e.g., 30–45 mins) with explainable delay causality (e.g., "heavy traffic on Mount Road"). The entire system operates 100% locally with zero paid API dependencies, zero Google Maps billing, and zero recurring cloud costs.

4 Unit Testing Architecture & 5-Layer Error Boundaries

The codebase enforces 5 defensive error containment boundaries tested by 63 automated unit and smoke tests passing with 100% success:

Layer 1: HTTP Boundary
Client & Request Validation

Catches unregistered routes (404), unhandled crashes (500), and invalid IDs with flash redirects.

Layer 2: Math Boundary
Algorithmic Invariants

Haversine domain protection clamps a ∈ [0, 1]; lower clamp ensures eta_low >= 5m; timestamp fallback.

Layer 3: DB Boundary
Relational & FK Integrity

Enforces PRAGMA foreign_keys = ON, rollback upon integrity errors, and cascading cleanups.

Layer 4: Notification Boundary
External Simulation & Phone Fallback

Non-blocking multi-channel dispatch; phone fallback handles missing/empty values; UTF-8 preserves regional scripts.

Layer 5: State Machine Boundary
Saturation Tolerance & Overrides

Cleanly flags unassigned when all specialists are busy; audits skill-mismatch manual overrides.

6 Viva Voce Defense & Evaluator Q&A Cheat Sheet

Q: Why communicate an uncertainty range instead of an exact single arrival minute?
A: A single-point time (e.g. "10:15 AM") presents false precision in urban traffic. When traffic or prior job complications delay arrival to 10:27 AM, customer trust is broken. Communicating an honest window [10:15–10:35 AM] accommodates natural transit variance without breaching expectations.
Q: How are cascading dwell-time overruns handled?
A: The autonomous event listener intercepts on-site delay reports (+15m), immediately propagates the delay to downstream appointments on the technician's sequence, expands uncertainty bands, and dispatches proactive SMS alerts to prevent WISMO calls.
Q: Why is this deterministic approach more appropriate than an opaque Neural Network?
A: Deep learning models cannot legally or operationally justify why a technician was 20 minutes late. Our deterministic model provides 100% explainability ("delayed due to heavy traffic on Mount Road"), full dispatcher override capability, and complete audit logging in assignment_history with zero recurring cloud cost.
